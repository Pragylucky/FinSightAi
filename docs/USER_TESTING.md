# User testing plan

Goal: replace the **Hypothesis** items in [PRODUCT.md](PRODUCT.md) with evidence. Five sessions is enough to find the biggest usability problems.

**Who:** 5 people who invest (or want to) but are not finance professionals. Friends, classmates, family; not people who've watched you build it.
**How:** 20 minutes each, in person or on a call with screen share. Open the live demo, give no instructions beyond the tasks below, and **don't help**. Note where they hesitate.

## Tasks (read aloud one at a time)

1. "You've heard Infosys is a good company. Find out whether you'd be comfortable investing in it."
2. "You're choosing between TCS and Wipro. Which looks stronger and why?"
3. "Find a few companies with strong returns on equity but a low valuation."
4. "Ask the assistant something you actually want to know about a stock."

## Questions after

- In your own words, what is this product for?
- When you read the AI analysis, did you trust it? What would make you trust it more or less?
- Did you notice "What is this based on?" Did it matter?
- What did you expect to find that wasn't there?
- Would you use this again? Instead of what?
- How easy was it, 1-5?

## After the sessions

- Count how many people completed each task unaided.
- List problems by how many people hit them.
- Fix the top 3 and add a **"What testing changed"** section to PRODUCT.md with before and after. That section is the most valuable thing you can show an interviewer.
